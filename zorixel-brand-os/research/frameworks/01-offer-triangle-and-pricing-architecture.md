# 📐 Framework 01: The Offer Triangle & High-Ticket Pricing Architecture

## 1. The Offer Triangle Mechanics
1. **What to Sell:** AI-Powered Web Experience & Component Sprints. AI tools perform 80% of delivery work, human judgment provides strategy and visual polish.
2. **Who to Sell To:** Able and willing buyers. B2B companies with real revenue ($500K–$5M ARR), not broke solopreneurs.
3. **Pricing Model:** Value-based pricing ($1,500 – $5,000/month or $3,000 14-day sprint). Never charge hourly.

## 2. The 3 Pricing Tiers
- **Foundation Tier:** $1,500/mo or setup fee.
- **Engine Tier (Recommended Start):** $3,000 for 14-Day Sprint ($1,500 deposit upfront).
- **Operator Tier:** $5,000–$10,000/mo retainer for full AIOS workflow automation & design systems.
