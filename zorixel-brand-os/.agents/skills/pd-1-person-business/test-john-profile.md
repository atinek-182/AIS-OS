After running the skill, the blueprint should arrive at roughly:

**Business Model:** B2B AI UGC Agency — pure AI-only delivery for new-age CPG brands

**ICP:** New-age CPG brands ($1M-$30M revenue, $20K+/month Meta + TikTok ad spend):
- Energy drinks (Alani Nu, Celsius, Ghost, Bloom tier)
- Creatine gummies + supplement gummies (Create, Bears, Olly tier)
- Functional beverages (Olipop, Magic Mind, adaptogens)
- Pre-workout / gym supplements (Ryze, Bucked Up tier)
- Decision-makers: Head of Growth, Head of Performance Marketing, CMO

**Offer Statement:**
"I help new-age CPG brands (energy drinks, creatine gummies, functional supplements) generate 25-30 high-performing AI UGC ad variations per month — tested live in their Meta + TikTok ad accounts to find 2-3 winning hooks driving down CPA — delivered for $5,000/month on a 3-month retainer."

**Pricing tiers:**
- Foundation: $2,500/mo (15 AI UGC ads/month)
- **Engine: $5,000/mo (25-30 ads/month)** ← recommended starting tier
- Operator: $7,500/mo (45+ ads/month + creative strategy + monthly performance reviews)

**AI Workflow (100% AI-only — perfect Arcads showcase):**
1. Claude analyzes brand's top-performing organic + best ads + ingredient claims + customer reviews → extracts brand voice + winning hooks
2. Claude writes 25-30 ad scripts/month using hook × actor type × pain point × CTA matrix
3. Arcads.ai generates the actual UGC videos (25-30 talking-head ads from 5-7 hooks × 4 AI actors × 2 CTAs). 100% AI — no shoots, no human creators.
4. Deliver weekly via Google Drive with launch recommendations
5. Weekly performance review with brand → feed winners back into Claude for next round

**Lead Gen:**
- Primary platform: LinkedIn (whitespace for AI UGC content + CPG marketer audience)
- Secondary: Instagram (portfolio + brand-side discovery)
- Outbound: LinkedIn DMs + cold email + Upwork (week 1-4 starter only)
- Realistic activity: 50-80 outbound messages/day after email warmup

**Monetization:**
- 3-month retainer minimum
- 50% deposit ($2,500) + 50% on month-1 delivery
- Upsell path: Engine → Operator → Performance Layer (months 4-12)
- 12-month lifetime client value: ~$90K-$110K per client

**Revenue math:**
- 1 Engine client = $5K/mo
- 2 Engine clients = $10K/mo (the goal)
- Timeline: first client days 45-75, second client days 75-120

## Notes on differences from Brian's profile

- **John has LESS brand portfolio** — that's why his starting tier is $5K (Engine) not $10K (Operator). More realistic for a first-time agency seller.
- **John has NO sub-creator network** — pure AI delivery only. This is the cleanest possible Arcads showcase.
- **John focuses on ONE niche** (new-age CPG) instead of three. Sharper positioning = easier sales pitch + better demo clarity.
- **John's timeline is 90-120 days to $10K/mo** (not 30-60). More honest expectation for someone starting cold.
- **John's outbound volume is 50-80/day** (not 60-100). Matches LinkedIn limits + email warmup reality.
- **Upwork is INCLUDED for John** (was excluded for Brian) — useful starter channel when you have no brand portfolio.
