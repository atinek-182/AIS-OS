### 🕒 1:45 - 3:15 | STAGE 3: LIVE NOTION CALL INTAKE & PROGRESS DEMO
*Visual: Screen Share (Showing Notion Intake Form & Progress Bar)*

- **How Call Intake Works:**
  1. Call ke waqt bas Scholar का name aur WhatsApp number type kariye.
  2. Multi-select checkboxes tick kariye (e.g. `[x] Ph.D. Writing (₹20,000)`, `[x] Patent Service (₹15,000)`).
  3. Enter Total Fee & Advance Received -> Notion auto-calculates Balance Due.
  4. Select Assigned Staff -> Click **Submit**.
- **The Live Magic:**
  *"Jaise hi aap Submit karenge, instant staff ke desktop board par unka checklist chala jayega. Jaise jaise staff Steps 1 to 5 tick karenge, aapko apne phone par live visual progress bar dikhega (`0%` -> `40%` -> `100%`) — bina kisi ko single call ya message kiye!"*
