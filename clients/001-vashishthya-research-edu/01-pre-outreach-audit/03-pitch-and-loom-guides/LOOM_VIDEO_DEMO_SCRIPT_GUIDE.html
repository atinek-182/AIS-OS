PART 2: STRATEGIC AUDIT & RECORDING RULES (WHAT NOT TO DO)

1. Never Apologize or Say "I am not here to sell anything":

- Saying "I am not here to sell anything or charge any fee" lowers your perceived authority and sounds defensive.

- Reframe with high authority: *"I built a custom, zero-subscription AI automation workspace specifically for your journal's daily operations to save your team 50+ hours every month."*

2. Never Show Blank Fields or Incomplete Cards:

- Saying *"I have not written the email address but you can add that as well"* makes the system look half-baked.

- Pre-populate all demo fields with realistic data before pressing record.

3. Never Highlight Lack of Internal Knowledge:

- Saying *"since I don't know about your internal work, I have not created this for employees..."* diminishes trust.

- Frame your setup as a flexible, modular framework: *"I've pre-configured the core stages of scholar intake. We can fine-tune your internal stage names during a brief 10-minute onboarding call."*

4. Use Crisp, Professional Terminology:

- Avoid mispronunciations or vague phrases like "anti-aggressive", "scrap", "this column".

- Use clear terms: *Notion Intake Portal*, *Google Scholar Indexing Meta-Tags*, *Automated Financial Reconciliation*, *1-Click WhatsApp Pre-filled Receipt*.

5. Focus on the Core Wins First:

- Highlight the 3 immediate operational victories first: Scholar Intake Form, 15-Minute Google Sheets Auto-Sync, 1-Click WhatsApp Balance Alert.

- Keep future features contained in a single, focused roadmap slide at the end.
