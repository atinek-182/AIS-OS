3. How to Get Your Shareable Notion Link

1-Click Template Link Generation

  1. Click Share in the top right corner of your Notion database page.
  2. Select the Publish tab -> Click Publish to web.
  3. Turn ON the toggle for Allow duplication as template.
  4. Click Copy web link. This link is what you show on Loom and send to the client!
