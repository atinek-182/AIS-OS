# 23-Sites Design Psychology, Sensory Feel, & Spatial Choreography Guide

*Synthesized from 23 award-winning reference sites: Active Theory, Obys Agency, Locomotive Agency, Merci Michel, Resn, Oryzo AI, Trionn Agency, and more.*

---

## 🧠 1. UX Design Psychology Principles

### A. Cognitive Load Optimization & Chunking
- **Micro-Copy Friction Reducers**: Never demand action without relieving friction. Place subtle micro-copy next to CTAs (*"No credit card required"*, *"Takes 2 minutes"*, *"Instant preview"*).
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Keep initial screen load focused on 1 headline, 1 value prop, and 1 primary CTA. Complex technical features belong in expandable accordions, tabbed drawers, or secondary bento cards.

### B. Eye-Tracking Scanning Patterns (F-Pattern & Z-Pattern)
- **Top-Left Anchor**: Logo and primary branding.
- **Top-Right Anchor**: Primary high-contrast CTA button.
- **Center Focus**: Main value proposition headline (3-line max, 48px–72px).
- **Bottom-Center Scroll Trigger**: Mouse indicator or subtle scroll arrow signaling below-the-fold content.

## 📐 2. Spatial Choreography & Section Sequencing

### The 4 Canonical Section Sequences

#### Archetype 1: AI SaaS / Product Launch Flow
```
1. [Floating Glass Nav]
2. [Hero: WebGL/Canvas Stage + High-Contrast Headline + Dual CTAs]
3. [Social Proof Marquee Ticker (Client logos / Press)]
4. [Problem / Agitation (2-Column Visual Contrast Grid)]
5. [Solution Bento (3x3 Modular Bento Cards with magnetic hover)]
6. [Interactive Specs / Live Sandbox Viewport]
7. [Pricing Matrix (3-Tier Card Comparison)]
8. [High-Contrast Action CTA Stage]
9. [Multi-Column Footer + Noise Texture]
```

#### Archetype 2: High-Ticket Agency / Portfolio Flow
```
1. [Minimal Header]
2. [Hero: Massive Typographic Headline + Video Mask]
3. [Manifesto / Philosophy Statement (Split-Text Scroll Reveal)]
4. [Selected Works (Fullscreen Interactive Canvas Stage)]
5. [Capability Bento Grid (Hover Video Previews)]
6. [Awards & Client Proof Matrix]
7. [Magnetic Project Inquiry CTA]
8. [Dark Mode Footer + Local Time Indicator]
```

## ✨ 3. Sensory "Feel" & Spatial Rhythm Rules

- **Obys Spatial Rhythm Ratio**:
  - Macro Section Vertical Padding: `120px` to `160px` on desktop, `64px` to `80px` on mobile.
  - Card Internal Spacing: `24px` to `32px`.
  - Element Gap in Grids: `16px` to `24px`.

- **Tension & Release Scroll Flow**:
  - Alternating section visual weights:
    - *Dense / Dark*: 3D Stage Hero (High tension).
    - *Clean / Light*: White/Light Gray Manifesto section (Release).
    - *Grid / Structured*: Dark Bento Feature Grid (Tension).
    - *Minimal / Clean*: Accordion FAQ & Testimonial slider (Release).

- **Tactile Feedback Physics**:
  - Micro-scale on hover: `scale(1.03)` with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing.
  - Magnetic pull distance: `max 20px` translation relative to cursor coordinates.
