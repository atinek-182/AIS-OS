# ZORIXEL Master Brand Identity v3.0 Reference Manual

## Core Brand Palette
- **Obsidian Midnight** (`#0D0E12`): Primary dark canvas background.
- **Warm Linen Cream** (`#FAF8F5`): Primary light canvas background.
- **Velvet Burgundy** (`#6D001A`): Primary brand color / header accents.
- **Electric Crimson** (`#D61C2C`): Primary CTA button & pop highlight.
- **Vintage Ochre Gold** (`#C5A059`): Accent ONLY (<5% surface area). Strictly prohibited as full slide background.

## Typography Hierarchy
- `Nuqun-Regular`: Official Logotype / Logo Mark ONLY (`projects/font-showcase/zorixel_logo_solid_white.svg`, `zorixel_logo_solid_dark.svg`). Never used for body or UI text.
- `Havock`: Primary Display Headline #1 (User's favorite display & hero title typeface).
- `Vixa`: Approved Display Heading & Poster Font.
- `Rosehot`: Official Subheadings & Editorial Tag Font (used for all headings apart from the main big headline).
- `AICON` (Bold & ExtraBold): Selective Section Accent Sans (used when matching technical section vibes).
- `Chorus-Black`: Approved for Zorixel Instagram posters & carousels.
- Non-Zorixel Client Vault Fonts: `Pavot`, `Queensides`, `Rozina-Bold`, `ST-Kosmolet`, `BiggerDisplay`, `Martius`, `Ortland`, `KalamaykaVF`, `SunsetHeavy`, `Medium-Font`, `VanArkel`.

## Zero-Gradient & Zero-Glassmorphism Rule
- Fills must be solid, crisp, and flat.
- Glassmorphism, backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur()`), semi-transparent glass cards, or glass borders are strictly prohibited.
