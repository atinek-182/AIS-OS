## Q&A Log

### Q1 — Primary Brand Category & Positioning Statement
- **Concept Explained**: Direct-Response Outcome vs. Engineering-Led Operations Positioning (Autoploy's black-box vs ZORIXEL's human-in-the-loop control UI).
- **Asked**: Which core positioning archetype best defines ZORIXEL's market strategy against horizontal competitors like Autoploy?
- **Captured Decision**: Option A — Production-Grade AI Operations & Custom Control Dashboards ("ZORIXEL builds client-owned AI operations systems with custom management dashboards, human approval controls, and zero vendor lock-in.")
- **Rationale & Trade-offs**: Directly solves the biggest trust gap in AI automation (black-box hallucinations and lack of control). Allows ZORIXEL to charge premium rates ($3k–$15k+) because it combines backend automation with custom React/Next.js UI interfaces.

### Q2 — Pricing Architecture & Micro-Service Proof Ladder
- **Concept Explained**: Staged Micro-Service Proof vs. Full Upfront Commitment (Nate Herk Masterclass Model). Overwhelming clients with a $10k+ full-system proposal creates friction and anxiety. Starting with 1–2 high-ROI micro-services establishes immediate trust, proves value, and naturally leads to higher-ticket expansion.
- **Asked**: How should ZORIXEL structure its pricing tiers and client offer boundaries?
- **Captured Decision**: **Staged Micro-Service Proof Ladder (Nate Herk Masterclass Model)**.
  - Start with 1–2 priority micro-service automations ($1.5k–$2.5k or value-proportional 10%–20% of 1st-year savings) to eliminate client anxiety and get fast ROI proof.
  - Measure 30-day performance baseline and present proof review.
  - Expand into Growth/Scale phases ($5k–$15k+) and monthly reliability/roadmap retainers ($400–$1,500/mo).

### Q3 — Client Acquisition Funnel & Intake Mechanics
- **Concept Explained**: Direct-Response VSL vs. Interactive Value-First Tool (Universal Client Acquisition Feature Blueprint).
- **Asked**: What primary intake and conversion mechanism should ZORIXEL use on its official client acquisition website?
- **Captured Decision**: **Option A — Hybrid Interactive Bottleneck & ROI Calculator + 3-Min Demonstration** (with open-ended addition of client-beneficial features).
  - Prospects type their custom business bottleneck in an open-ended field.
  - Live ROI engine displays estimated annual labor hours & dollar savings without exposing upfront price tags.
  - Prospect claims a 1-on-1 personalized Loom Video Audit & Notion proposal.
  - All lead data automatically syncs to Google Sheets CRM (`gws`).
  - *Expansion Note*: Designed modularly to easily add more high-value, client-beneficial interactive micro-tools as ZORIXEL scales.
- **Rationale & Trade-offs**: Positions ZORIXEL as an interactive, evidence-first engineering firm rather than another generic VSL sales page.

### Q4 — Handover Infrastructure & Code Ownership Policy
- **Concept Explained**: Vendor Lock-in vs. 100% Client-Owned Handover (Nate Herk Masterclass & ZORIXEL Ownership Mandate).
- **Asked**: What infrastructure handover and code ownership policy should ZORIXEL enforce for all client builds?
- **Captured Decision**: **Option A — 100% Transparent Client Ownership Package**.
  - Production utilities (Anthropic/OpenAI API keys, Supabase DB, Vercel hosting) sit strictly in the client's account and on the client's payment card.
  - Complete transfer of clean GitHub repository (Next.js UI dashboard code, workflow schemas, Zod validators).
  - Delivered with Video SOP runbooks, architecture diagrams, and 30-day success review.
  - Zero vendor lock-in; client has full commercial rights and independence.
- **Rationale & Trade-offs**: Eliminates agency billing liability/token risk, creates immediate trust with technical buyers, and serves as a major strategic differentiator against agencies that lock clients into proprietary retainers.
