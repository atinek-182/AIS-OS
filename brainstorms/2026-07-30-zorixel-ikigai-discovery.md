## 🔒 Locked Business Blueprint for ZORIXEL

### 1. Locked Niche Statement
> *"I help growth-stage AI SaaS founders and agency owners replace ugly, generic websites with high-converting, micro-interactive web experiences and instant <5-minute lead response automations — delivered in 14 days for $3,000."*

### 2. The Offer Architecture ($3,000 Sprint)
- **Core Deliverable:** 5–8 Page Responsive Web App / Landing Page built with locked OKLCH tokens, custom typography (`Rosehot`/`Nuqun` + `Outfit`), and GSAP ScrollTrigger micro-interactions.
- **Speed-to-Lead Engine:** Automated lead intake form with instant <5-minute personalized AI email response & CRM database sync.
- **Quality Assurance:** 5-Viewport Playwright Visual Audit Report (Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, Desktop, Ultrawide).
- **Payment Structure:** 50% upfront deposit ($1,500) upon agreement signature; 50% upon final launch ($1,500).

### 3. The 100% Async Sales Flow
1. **Prospect List:** Find 20 target AI SaaS founders / agency owners per day on LinkedIn / X.
2. **Loom Video Audit:** Send a 2-minute screen recording showing 1 generic component on their site and showing a live interactive redesign prototype.
3. **Notion Proposal:** Send a 1-page Notion proposal with deposit link.
4. **Deposit & Kickoff:** Collect $1,500 deposit before writing code!

### 4. Revenue Math to $10,000 / Month Goal
- **1 Sale / Month** = $3,000 USD (~₹2,50,000 INR) $\rightarrow$ Validates model & covers all living costs.
- **2 Sales / Month** = $6,000 USD (~₹5,000,000 INR) $\rightarrow$ Full-time agency income.
- **3 Sales / Month** = **$9,000 – $10,500 USD (~₹8.5 Lakhs/month)** $\rightarrow$ **$10K/mo Goal Achieved!**
